Weekday morning digest. 1) Read today's calendar: list each event with its start time, title and anything to prepare. Flag overlaps and meetings with no agenda. 2) Read unread mail from the last 24 hours. Group it into "needs a reply today", "worth reading" and "can wait", one line per message with sender and subject. 3) Post a short digest (under 200 words) to the person's usual chat: today's schedule first, then the mail groups. 4) Print the same digest to stdout so the scheduler's history shows what was produced. Read only: never send, reply to, forward, archive or delete any mail, and never accept, decline, create or move any calendar event. If something looks urgent, say so in the digest and leave the action to the person.
